### Elements 
- Target analysis: Industry, Business, Performance
- Market analysis: Macro, Market Sentiment
- Investor mindset: Principles, Objectivity (avoiding biases)
    * Acting according to principles in a disciplined manner 
    * Clear criteria for success and failure 
        - profit-to-loss ratio: 1) accept loss if failure, 2) earn big, lose small
        - if cash available, will you buy your current holdings at the current situation?
    * Continuously refining the investment process and principles for long-term returns
    * 투자전에 이미 내 생각이 틀렸다고 전제하고 대응방안 수립 (e.g., 최대 Loss 규정)
    * Trap in the target price: 목표주가에 도달하면 매각을 시사함, 도달시 목표주가는 Revise 되어야 함
- (in addition) Risk Management, Portfolio Management
